# E-commerce Sales & Customer Analytics
## Business Insights Report

### 1. Sales Performance
- November 2017 recorded the highest monthly merchandise revenue at approximately R$987,765.
- The same month recorded 7,289 delivered orders, the highest monthly order volume in the analysed period.
- Average Order Value in November 2017 was R$135.51, suggesting that the revenue peak was primarily associated with higher order volume rather than unusually high spending per order.

### 2. Geographical Performance
- São Paulo (SP) generated approximately R$5.07 million in delivered-order merchandise revenue, making it the leading state.
- Rio de Janeiro (RJ) and Minas Gerais (MG) ranked second and third.
- The business has an opportunity to investigate growth potential in states outside its strongest market.

### 3. Product Category Performance
- Health & Beauty was the highest-revenue category, generating approximately R$1.23 million.
- Watches & Gifts ranked second with approximately R$1.17 million.
- Bed, Bath & Table recorded the highest order count among the top categories, with 9,272 orders.
- The top three categories contributed approximately 25.89% of total merchandise revenue.

### 4. Customer Retention
- Approximately 97% of customers made only one delivered purchase.
- Only around 3% of customers made repeat purchases.
- Repeat customers had average cumulative merchandise spending of R$260.05, compared with R$137.96 for one-time customers.
- Repeat customers therefore showed approximately 88% higher average cumulative spending.

### 5. Delivery Performance
- 89.15% of all orders were classified as delivered on time.
- 7,827 orders were classified as late.
- 2.98% had unknown delivery status.
- Among orders with known delivery status, approximately 8.12% were late.

### 6. Customer Satisfaction
- The average review score was 4.09 out of 5.
- Approximately 77% of recorded reviews had scores of 4 or 5.
- Approximately 14.6% had scores of 1 or 2.
- Further investigation could identify whether late deliveries are associated with negative customer reviews.

### 7. Delivery Delays and Customer Satisfaction

- Orders delivered on time received an average review score of 4.29/5.
- Late deliveries received an average score of only 2.57/5.
- Approximately 54% of reviewed late deliveries received negative ratings (1 or 2).
- Orders with unknown delivery status had an average rating of 1.75/5.

**Business recommendation:** Investigate delivery delays and improve logistics coordination, particularly for sellers and regions with recurring late deliveries.

**Analytical note:** The results show an association between delivery status and review scores, not proof of causation. Orders with unknown delivery status require separate investigation.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Improve customer retention:** Explore loyalty programmes, personalised offers and post-purchase engagement campaigns.
2. **Strengthen delivery performance:** Investigate late deliveries by seller, state and product category.
3. **Expand geographical reach:** Evaluate marketing opportunities in states beyond São Paulo.
4. **Optimise product strategy:** Study pricing, demand and profitability across leading product categories.
5. **Improve customer experience:** Investigate negative reviews and identify recurring service or delivery issues.

## Methodology and Notes

- Analysis uses the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset.
- Revenue figures represent merchandise revenue and exclude freight charges.
- Customer purchase behaviour is based on delivered orders.
- Repeat-customer spending represents cumulative historical merchandise spending in the dataset, not average spending per order.
- Delivery percentages use all orders unless explicitly stated otherwise.
- The dataset covers historical transactions, primarily from 2016 to 2018.
- Observed relationships indicate business opportunities but do not establish causation.
